Our Story

We started with a sense of adventure. My husband and I in earlier times made it our goal to visit ALL of the California Pacific Coast. We spent our vacations in the luxury of sandy tents in hot summer days. Waking up to the ever-changing bird songs, my husband would jump into the startling water. I am more of a morning light-stretch yoga person myself.

In later times our goals have changed as ourselves. The great outdoors is not as forgiving to our backs as it once was. A nice cozy bed or a heated pool at the end of a strenous day was not so much of a luxury to us anymore but peace of mind for the reality of tomorrow. The Pacific North Coast became our home. Pacific Trails Resort was founded out of our own needs and a new goal to sharing our home with other adventurers. Please enjoy the same trails that we wandered and the same waters that shaped us.
