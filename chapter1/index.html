My favorite coffee is a regular capuccino. About 2 years ago I bought an espresso machine and making it takes about 5 minutes. I wouldn't call it a sweet drink, but that's why I like since it has no syrups added masking the taste of the coffee. I purchase the coffee beans at various local coffee roasters as whole beans. Different coffee roasters make different notes of flavor so I like driving all around town to find good ones. I typically drink it in the morning before work. I would recommend homemade capuccino/coffee, it has really hone in what flavor I like my coffee to taste like.
